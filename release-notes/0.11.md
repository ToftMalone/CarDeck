## What's new

- 🛞 **Wheelspins per trip**: every recorded trip now counts how many times the driven wheels spun (the speed climbing faster than the car can actually accelerate). The number is shown with the trip's duration and distance, and added up when trips are merged.
- ✨ **Smoother animations**: the dashboard, diagnosis and trip selection fade from one state to the next instead of switching abruptly. Buttons sink slightly when touched. The App – OBD2 – ECU strip fills in as the connection comes up.
- 📜 **Trip panel**: drag it up or down with your finger. It slides up from the bottom once the trip is loaded.
- 🐢 **Faster long lists**: the trip history and the vehicle picker only build the rows on screen, so they stay smooth even with hundreds of trips. Searching fault codes no longer slows typing down.
- 🔠 **Text that fits**: long translated text ends with "…" instead of being cut off, and buttons, chips and cards grow with Android's larger font sizes.
- 🧹 **Polish**: the vehicle list slides in the right direction when going back, the top bar tints on scroll on every page, the empty trip history matches the other screens, and the French text consistently uses the formal "vous".
