## What's new

- 🌍 **CarDeck now speaks English, German, Russian and Spanish** as well as French. The app follows your phone's language (English if it isn't available). On Android 13 and later you can also pick CarDeck's language on its own in Android settings.
- 🚗 **Many more vehicles**: 10 new brands (Chevrolet, Jaguar, Lancia, Land Rover, Lexus, MG, Porsche, Saab, Smart, Subaru), about 140 new models and generations, including the Porsche 911 997 GT3 MK1 and MK2.
- 🤝 **New Contribution tile** on the home screen: explore your car, then send the findings to the developer in a few taps so your car can be fully supported. CarDeck prepares an anonymised file (no VIN serial number, no nickname) with a complete vehicle sheet, and opens a pre-filled GitHub issue.
- ⚙️ **Settings are back** as a gear icon at the top right of the home screen.
