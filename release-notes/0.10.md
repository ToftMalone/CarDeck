## What's new

- 💥 **Faster knock search** (Suzuki Swift Sport ZC33S): the main engine data block is now read on every round, and alone during full-load accelerations, instead of once every 6 to 7 seconds. Frozen blocks are only read now and then. The screen shows how many engine reads were taken under load, so you know when the recording has enough data.
