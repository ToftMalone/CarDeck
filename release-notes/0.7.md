## What's new

- 🚗 **304 more vehicles**: 12 new brands (BYD, Cadillac, Chrysler, Daihatsu, Dodge, Ferrari, Infiniti, Isuzu, Lada, Lamborghini, Maserati, SsangYong), many new models (Peugeot 106 / 306 / 406 / 607, Citroën Saxo / Xsara / C2, Volkswagen Scirocco / Touareg / Amarok, Toyota Supra / GT86 / Land Cruiser, Ford S-Max…) and older or newer generations of existing models (Clio I, Golf III, Passat B6 / B7, Astra H, 3008 III…). The list now has over 2,000 engines from 48 brands. The Porsche 911 997 GT3 was added in the meantime.
- 🔧 **"Dedicated profile" is now "Reverse-engineered"**: the badge shown on vehicles whose manufacturer data CarDeck has decoded itself.
- 🌍 **Translation fix**: some translated texts could fail to display on certain phones.
