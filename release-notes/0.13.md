## What's new

- ⏱️ **Performance**: a new page, in place of the OBD2 adapter tile on the home screen. It times **0 to 100 km/h**, **400 m standing start** (with the speed at the line), **50 to 90 km/h** and **80 to 120 km/h**, and **braking from 100 to 0 km/h** (stopping distance and time). The timer starts on its own, and while the page is open CarDeck reads only the speed, as fast as the adapter allows. The number of readings per second is shown, as it sets the accuracy. Each run is saved per vehicle with its conditions (outside and intake air temperature, barometric pressure, fuel), along with the best result of each test. Only on a track or a road closed to traffic.
- 🚗 **Edit a vehicle**: the pencil in Settings → Garage now opens a full page: name, plate, year, model (with the vehicle picker) and the OBD2 adapter (pair another one or unpair it). Trips and diagnoses are kept.
- 🏠 The home screen tiles now stay in a fixed order.
- 🤝 Contribution: sending findings through a GitHub issue has been removed. Exploration and "Share results" remain.
- 🛞 Wheelspins are no longer shown in the trip details (they are still counted).
